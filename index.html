FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Everything you need to know about working with Lumina — from onboarding to data sovereignty.

How long does a typical integration take?

Most enterprise integrations are production-ready within 4–6 weeks. We connect directly to your existing stack — PostgreSQL, SAP, Salesforce, or custom APIs — with pre-built connectors that eliminate months of custom development. A dedicated solutions architect is assigned from day one.

Where is our data stored? Is it truly sovereign?

Your data never leaves the region you choose. We deploy on AWS (Bahrain), Azure (Dubai), or on-premises inside your own data centre. Every model is trained and served within your security perimeter — zero data leakage, full GDPR and SOC 2 Type II compliance.

What industries do you serve?

We work across logistics, e-commerce, banking, manufacturing, healthcare, and government. Our platform is industry-agnostic — the AI models adapt to your domain-specific data, whether you're optimising fleet routes, predicting equipment failure, or automating document workflows.

Do we need a data science team to use Lumina?

Not at all. Lumina's dashboards and automated workflows are designed for business users — no SQL or Python required. For teams that do have technical depth, we expose full API access and Jupyter notebook integrations so your data scientists can build on top of our platform.

How is pricing structured?

We offer annual contracts based on the number of data sources connected and the product modules you choose. There are no per-seat fees — your entire organisation gets access. Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute demo and fit assessment with zero commitment.

Can we try Lumina before committing?

Absolutely. We offer a 4-week paid proof of concept where we connect your real data, build a live dashboard, and deliver measurable KPIs. If the results don't meet the agreed benchmarks, you walk away — no lock-in, no hard feelings.
